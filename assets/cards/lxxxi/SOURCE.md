# LXXXI Quareia Magician's Deck Card Faces

Local JPEG images for offline use in this site.

- Deck: LXXXI — Quareia The Magician's Deck (奎瑞亚魔法牌)
- Files: `lxxxi-01.jpeg` … `lxxxi-81.jpeg` (card numbers 1–81)
- Card back: `lxxxi-back.jpeg` (shared by all face-down LXXXI cards in the UI)

Card meanings in `js/lxxxi-data.js` are extracted from the official companion
booklet 《LXXXI 奎瑞亞魔法牌說明書》（繁體中文版） and rendered in simplified
Chinese for this site.

Card art © Josephine McCarthy, Stuart Littlejohn, Cassandra Beanland. Do not
redistribute the original master files or claim the art as original work. See
