The public source repository does not distribute LXXXI card images. The website
loads low-resolution WebP display derivatives from the versioned resource base:

`https://assets.luotianyi.fun/tarot-divination-site/lxxxi/v1`

- Card back: `backs/lxxxi-back.webp`

booklet 《LXXXI 奎瑞亞魔法牌說明書》（繁體中文版） and rendered in simplified
Chinese for this site. Layout information was compiled or translated from the
companion booklet.
