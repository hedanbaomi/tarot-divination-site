# LXXXI — Quareia The Magician's Deck

The public source repository does not distribute LXXXI card images. The website
loads low-resolution WebP display derivatives from the versioned resource base:

`https://assets.luotianyi.fun/tarot-divination-site/lxxxi/v1`

- Card faces: `cards/lxxxi-01.webp` … `cards/lxxxi-81.webp`
- Card back: `backs/lxxxi-back.webp`

Card meanings in `js/lxxxi-data.js` are extracted from the official companion
booklet 《LXXXI 奎瑞亞魔法牌說明書》（繁體中文版） and rendered in simplified
Chinese for this site. Layout information was compiled or translated from the companion
booklet.

Quareia identifies LXXXI as a collaboration between Josephine McCarthy, Stuart
Littlejohn, and Cassandra Beanland:

- https://www.quareia.com/
- https://www.quareia.com/how-to
